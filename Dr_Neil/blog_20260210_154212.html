The Importance of Regular Dental Checkups

One of the cornerstones of maintaining oral health is attending regular dental checkups. These visits allow Dr. Neil to monitor the health of your teeth and gums, catch potential issues early, and provide professional cleanings that enhance your at-home routine. Remember, prevention is always better than cure!

Mastering Oral Hygiene: Daily Habits for Success

Consistent daily habits are crucial for maintaining oral health. Dr. Neil recommends brushing your teeth at least twice a day with fluoride toothpaste and flossing daily to remove plaque that can lead to decay and gum disease. In addition, using an antibacterial mouthwash can help reduce bacteria and keep your breath fresh.
