Innovative Treatments

Orthodontics is not just about aligning teeth; it’s about enhancing quality of life. Dr. Yang offers a variety of innovative treatments that go beyond traditional braces, including:

  • Invisalign®: A transparent, convenient alternative to metal braces, suitable for both teens and adults seeking a discreet way to achieve a perfect smile.
  • Lingual Braces: These braces are placed on the inside of the teeth, making them invisible to others while effectively correcting alignment.
  • Ceramic Braces: Aesthetic braces that blend with the natural color of teeth, offering a less noticeable alternative to traditional metal braces.
