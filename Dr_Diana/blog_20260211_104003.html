Introduction to Pediatric Orthodontics

Pediatric orthodontics is a specialized field of dentistry focused on diagnosing and treating dental issues in children. Early orthodontic care can play a crucial role in a child’s oral health, ensuring the correct development of the jaw and alignment of teeth. Dr. Diana is passionate about providing comprehensive orthodontic care tailored to the unique needs of her younger patients.

Why Early Orthodontic Evaluation is Important

According to the American Association of Orthodontists, children should have their first orthodontic screening by age seven. Early evaluation allows Dr. Diana to detect potential problems early, offering the opportunity to guide dental development proactively, and reduce the complexity of future treatments.

  • Early Detection: Identifying issues such as crowding, spacing, or bite anomalies early.
  • Guiding Growth: Taking advantage of the child's natural growth patterns for optimal results.
  • Reducing Treatment Complexity: Potentially minimizing the need for more extensive procedures in the future.

Common Orthodontic Issues in Children

Children can experience various orthodontic challenges, including:

  1. Overcrowded Teeth
  2. Excessive Spacing
  3. Crossbite, Overbite, and Underbite
  4. Protruding Teeth
  5. Jaw Growth Problems

Dr. Diana employs a range of techniques to address these issues, using state-of-the-art technology to ensure effective and comfortable treatments.

Orthodontic Treatment Options for Children

Dr. Diana offers a variety of treatment options suitable for children, including:

  • Braces: Traditional braces are highly effective for correcting alignment issues.
  • Invisalign First: Specially designed for young mouths in the early stages of orthodontic treatment.
  • Palatal Expanders: These devices help widen the upper jaw to make room for crowded teeth.

Each treatment is customized to fit the individual needs of the child for the best long-term outcomes.
